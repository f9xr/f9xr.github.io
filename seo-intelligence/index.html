How to read this: numbers shown in KPIs and tables are actual Google Search Console data for the exported window. Columns labelled benchmark or potential are modelled estimates (clearly marked) — not measured Google data. Anything we cannot verify is labelled a hypothesis.

Ranking & CTR opportunities

The opportunity model (transparent): for each query we estimate a Benchmark CTR from its average position using a standard industry CTR-vs-position curve (). Potential clicks = Impressions × max(0, Benchmark CTR − Actual CTR). This is a directional estimate of clicks you could recover by lifting CTR/position to benchmark — it is not measured Google data and it is not a traffic forecast.
